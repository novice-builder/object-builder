          How to play
          

            Left stick: walk · drag the world: look · ↑: jump.
Door
            picture: open / close. Table picture: craft.
Hammer: build ·
            choose a piece · +: place · −: remove your pieces · ↶: undo.
          

          

            Desktop: WASD / arrows, drag to look, Space jump, E use, B build, F
            place, R remove, Z undo. Esc closes a menu.
          

          

            There are no voice recordings yet. The speaker replays a written
            hint. Progress belongs to this browser / file location. Export a
            backup before moving the game.
          

          

            This game contains its renderer and art. It makes no network
            requests.
