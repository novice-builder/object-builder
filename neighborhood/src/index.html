            How to play
            

              Left stick: walk · drag the world: look · ↑: jump.

              Choose a hotbar picture, then tap a spot to build. Swipe the bar
              for more.

              Tap the selected material again to deselect. Tap a door or table
              to use it. Hold a built piece to put it away. ↶: undo.

              Furniture: tap a spot, rotate the preview, then tap the tick.

              Doors open automatically on approach unless switched off in
              Settings. Edge protection starts on. Use its small gameplay toggle
              to change it. Jump deliberately to leave an edge. Climbing is
              disabled.
            

            

              Desktop: WASD / arrows, drag to look, Space jump, E use, 0 empty
              hand, 1–7 pieces, F place, R remove (or rotate a preview), Shift+R
              fine rotation, Enter confirm, Z undo. Esc cancels a preview or
              opens/closes the menu.
            

            

              There are no voice recordings yet. The speaker replays a written
              hint. Neighborhood 06 saves separately from 05 and earlier
              versions. Your previous builds are copied on first opening when
              this browser can find them. Progress belongs to this browser /
              file location. Export a backup before moving the game.
            

            

              This game contains its renderer and art. It makes no network
              requests.
