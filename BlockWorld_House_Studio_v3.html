House Studio v3 · draw & trim

Start on an empty one-metre grid. Tap points to draw connected walls, add doors and windows during sketching, and select or trim individual wall sections. Generate the 3D view only when requested; bake the final mesh at 10 cm.

Start with any grid point. Continue around the house and tap the first point to close its outer boundary. Then draw internal walls. Select once for a whole wall or twice for the section between junctions.

3 · Convert and export

Bake samples your finished house into 10 cm solid cells, removes interior cube faces, and merges adjacent visible faces into larger mesh rectangles. It does not export individual cubes.
