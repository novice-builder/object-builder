300 × 200 m DXF map · 72 lots + roads

BlockWorld — playable import test

Mobile: move with the left joystick; drag the right side of the view to look. Aim with the centre crosshair, then press PLACE or DESTROY. Tap the size button to cycle 10, 20 and 40 cm.

Desktop: WASD or arrows to walk; click and drag to look, right click to use your selected tool, space to jump, mouse wheel in Orbit to zoom. Use Map for an overhead layout.

Every enclosed lot in the DXF gets a copy of your original Fusion shed and water tank. Smooth, block-style and hybrid houses alternate. The road and grass textures are generated locally, not downloaded images.

The HTML needs internet access to load Three.js. Everything else is embedded.
