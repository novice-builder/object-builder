BlockWorld 2.0 development prototype

New Home/Square layout, low-poly buildings, furniture, avatar/animal/vehicle geometry and icon.svg were authored originally during this task. No commercial reference-game assets were extracted or included. Emoji and system fonts are rendered by the device.

Run 1 adds the original Sunbeam Town layout, curated catalogue models and procedural surface patterns, raster installation icons derived from icon.svg, and distinct dog/cat/duck/horse geometry and animation. All artwork is generated by local geometry/canvas code; no external asset download is required at runtime.

Neighborhood 06 mechanics and compatibility world are preserved from novice-builder/object-builder commit df958096a88254bf611a64798e317ecb9fc3f5f9. The package records exact upstream file hashes in docs/LEGACY-SOURCES.json.
