# Contextual play and furniture

Use now selects a nearby visible target and shows its pictorial action: doors/windows/gates, lights, seating, cupboards, vehicles, neighbours/animals and play equipment. Wall/floor occlusion prevents reaching through a building. Swing, seesaw, trampoline, slide, spring pony and rocking chair have short, bounded play sequences; movement, Use or Jump can leave safely. Built equipment remains in the command/save state while its temporary animation runs. Edits are suppressed or safely end play, so history cannot leave a ghost object.

Authored region lamps and dining hutches can be toggled. Cupboard leaves have an obstruction-tested outward sweep; new object state remains undoable. Authored state is separately persisted by stable ID. Saving during play or sitting stores a safe entry pose; standing beside an upper-floor seat retains that floor rather than returning downstairs. Lights change their visible lamp state; bounded nearby illumination is follow-up performance work.

Native snapshot schema 3 reads schema 1 and 2, preserves legacy source bytes, and validates prop state. Layout revision 1 retains earlier Run 2 region geometry; revision 2 adds the functional cupboard placement. Existing Sunbeam Town/Home/Square geometry and player constructions are unchanged. New native worlds use schema 3; the historical Run 1 app cannot read them and remains available as its original archive.

Integration testing found excessive whole-world collision scanning during navigation. Incremental player-build collision indexing and local simulation queries were brought forward from Stage F. Objects are queried by their extent, so large furniture cannot be missed because its anchor is outside a fixed radius. The 12,000-piece capacity check confirms a small local result set and correct updates/removal.

Executed: catalogue/command/persistence/region suites passed; seven focused interaction/layout/cache cases passed. Seven Chromium scenarios passed for four touch activities/exit/save, authored light/cupboard state, seats, original/farm greetings, driving and upstairs/map/old-save compatibility. Initial failures found a cupboard against a partition, full-world scanning and identity-based filtering after indexing; each was fixed and the full focused browser run passed. No physical-device or sustained frame-rate claim is made.
