# Home & Square integrated prototype

Historical foundation slice. Run 1 adds [Sunbeam Town](SUNBEAM.md), the expanded [catalogue](CATALOGUE.md), [seating/living world](LIFE.md) and touch object relocation. The original Home/Square template below remains selectable with its original layout; old feature-limit statements describe the foundation checkpoint.

Stage 4 implements a compact original town: home/garage, café, library, workshop, gardens and a decorative pond. All four building centres and all five doorways are exercised by deterministic traversal checks. Furnishings use original geometry; windows are stylized opaque panes. This is not the full R1 town/content release.

The prototype has first/third-person camera toggle, third-person obstruction checks, collision-aware creative flight (24 m ceiling), seven unlimited original catalogue entries, a category-grid catalogue, four walking neighbours, two friendly animal models, two road-traffic cars and one drivable buggy. The map supports dragging, zoom buttons and safe landmark travel. World instances, build edits, camera/flight preferences and parked vehicle positions are saved.

Keyboard: WASD/arrows move, drag looks, Space jumps (or ascends in flight), Shift descends, E uses/enters/leaves, F places at crosshair, R rotates preview/removes, Enter confirms, Z/Y undo/redo. Touch: joystick, view drag, Jump, pictorial hotbar, long-press removal, preview rotate/confirm, camera/flight icons and dedicated flight up/down holds. Tap Use near the buggy to drive; park/leave before building. Home and map travel return to walking. Reload resumes on foot if the saved player would occupy a vehicle.

Ambient activity uses deliberately small bounded routes, local obstruction checks and no navigation-mesh/pathfinding framework. It is a foundation: complex rerouting, traffic junction rules, richer animal species/animations, riding, dialogue, seats, avatar wardrobe, audio and the 190-item launch catalogue remain incomplete. Animals and pedestrians greet through simple feedback rather than elaborate interactions. There is no combat, survival, collection, monetization or unlock progression.

The traffic loop is a simple perimeter route; the buggy uses kinematic collision and simple steering, not vehicle simulation. Four buildings and seven catalogue entries are accurately reported rather than treated as full R1 completion. Physical Lenovo/iPhone performance and Safari/WebKit remain unqualified.
