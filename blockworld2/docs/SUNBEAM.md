# Sunbeam Town

New `sunbeam-town` template; existing `home-square` instances retain their original four-building layout. No template is silently enlarged over a child's saved creation.

Delivered: 12 furnished main buildings, 3 usable roller-door garages, 4 clear building plots and 16 map destinations. The original home, café, library and workshop are joined by Sunbeam cottage, Bluebell house, Orchard home, school, clinic, bakery, garden studio and stables. Interiors use the expanded original furniture registry. Roads, approach paths, framed windows, signs, park equipment, orchard rows, gardens and edge landscaping form a roughly 140 m neighbourhood.

Every main interior has a clear tested centre; all 15 doorways pass approach/open/passage checks. Garages are checked against the actual buggy dimensions. Roof profiles use solid matching stepped geometry. Four building plots remain unobstructed, and ordinary creative placement remains available elsewhere subject to collision checks.

The town is a larger coherent preview, not the finished 320 m R1 world. Current buildings are single-storey; swimming, airport/aircraft, horse riding, more elaborate activities and physical-device performance qualification remain future releases. Original Home/Square and Legacy 06 are still selectable.
