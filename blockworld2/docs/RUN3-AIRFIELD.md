Walk up the ramp, through the real door opening and choose the front passenger seat with Use. There is no teleport-only boarding shortcut. The floor/ramp and interior use the same audited walking/collision system as buildings. Opaque aircraft parts are baked into one draw call, retaining glass, ramp and propellers separately.

The runway/hangar can be freely explored; there is no unlock or objective.
