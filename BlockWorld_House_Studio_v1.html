House Studio v1 · touch-first

Sketch at real dimensions. The optional 10 cm voxel → greedy mesh conversion happens only when you choose Bake. The source plan stays editable.

1 · Floor plan

Drag corner dots to reshape. Tap a wall to select it, then use Door or Window to position an opening.

The plan is freely editable; snapping is not required. For a new outline, tap its corners in order and then press Close outline.

2 · House controls and 3D preview

Selected opening · edit position and size

Tap a door or window in the plan to select it.

Drag the 3D view to orbit. Pinch to zoom, or use the + and − buttons. All measurements are metres.

3 · Convert and export

Bake samples your finished house into 10 cm solid cells, removes interior cube faces, and merges adjacent visible faces into larger mesh rectangles. It does not export individual cubes.

Ready. Draw or edit the house before baking.

Prototype limits: roof geometry is a simple flat or gable roof; the preview shows a bounding-box gable for nonrectangular plans, while the baked roof is clipped to the footprint. Bake limit: 650,000 sample cells to protect phones. Exported OBJ + MTL are for testing; map-studio import integration is a later step.
