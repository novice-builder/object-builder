1 · Recognition

Automatically detects long heavy horizontal and vertical strokes. Thin furniture lines and curved door swings are usually excluded. Inspect the overlay for errors.

2 · Dimension calibration

Choose Measure, then tap two walls in the floor-plan preview. Enter their real-world separation. The complete wall network rescales together, preserving connections and snapping to 100 mm.

Select two walls to calibrate.

After calibration, selected wall endpoints can be nudged together on the shared grid.

3 · House construction

Roof footprint is estimated from the outer extents. Complex L-shaped and multi-pitch roofs need correction; they aren't reliably inferred from a plan without roof elevations.

4 · Openings & corrections

Choose a wall, then add a door or window opening centered on that wall. Doors and windows are manual corrections in this version; curved symbols are not yet reliably classified.

Experimental image-to-geometry recognition. Best with high-contrast architectural plans. Geometry is illustrative, not suitable for construction measurements.
