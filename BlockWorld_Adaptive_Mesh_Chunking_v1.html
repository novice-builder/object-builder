What this demonstrates

All 38 original block-style prefabs, their tank geometry, the roads and grass are compiled into reusable full-detail geometry sections. No voxelisation, greedy merging, or 10/20/40 cm geometry conversion happens while you walk.

All distances use the same 10 cm coordinates. The entire hierarchy was baked before this HTML was generated. Only the mesh grouping changes.
